About the Studio

About RK Lumora Studio

RK Lumora Studio is an art and heritage conservation service dedicated to the careful care of artworks, books, paper objects, and cultural heritage materials. The studio works with private collectors, museums, galleries, libraries, archives, temples, and international clients who value responsible, well-documented conservation practice.

Every object is treated as unique. Proposed treatments always depend on a thorough examination of the object's condition, materials, and history — never on assumptions.

“Conservation is not about making an object look new. It is about stabilizing it, respecting its history, and ensuring it survives for the people who come after us.”

— Conservation philosophy, RK Lumora Studio

How We Work

Conservation Process

Pricing and timelines depend on the object, its condition, materials, and treatment requirements. Every engagement follows the same careful path.

  1. 01

    Initial Inquiry

    Contact the studio via WhatsApp or email and share photographs and details of your object.

  2. 02

    Preliminary Condition Discussion

    We discuss the object's history, visible condition, and your goals for its care.

  3. 03

    Assessment & Quotation

    The object is examined and a written assessment, treatment proposal, and quotation are prepared.

  4. 04

    Agreed Work & Documentation

    Once you approve the proposal, conservation work proceeds with full condition documentation.
